## Stage 16J Equal Earth outcome

Stage 16J implements the published spherical Equal Earth forward/reverse
equations in standalone C++ and dependency-free JavaScript without extending
the closed six-family release runtime. Its neutral fixture bundle contains 30
cases across the canonical Greenwich registration and an explicitly
experimental `11.5°E` Africa-centered variant. Frozen PROJ 9.6.2 and D3 Geo
2.0.1 observations agree to a maximum raw difference of
`7.021666937153402e-16`; the finite-difference spherical area audit has a
maximum error of `1.203175781228083e-9` over 35 samples.

The [one-to-five comparison record](equal-earth-positioning-speculations-v01.md)
renders:

1. a matched Web Mercator and canonical Equal Earth full-world control;
2. canonical and Africa-centered Equal Earth with Tissot diagnostics;
3. one Africa–Europe WGS 84 source-window slice through three registrations;
4. Equal Earth beside AuthaGraph, Dymaxion, and Myriahedral Afro–Eur–Asia; and
5. full-carrier, geographic-preclip, registered-perspective, and
   Cahill–Keyes native-cell slice roles.

The five 2560 × 1440 PNGs are checked local exploration products and are
registered in `make all-experiments`. Equal Earth is not in `make all`, the
standard artifact catalog, print generation, GitHub release, or UCB AAO/S3.
The centered variant is not labeled EPSG:8857. Mathematical implementation
does not establish `#CorrectTheMap` compliance, corrected perception,
community authorization, or a decolonial outcome.

## GPU benchmark contract

Every 16A–16D run emits a machine-readable result and compact human summary.
It records:

- input/product hashes; encoder, decoder, browser/runtime, driver, operating
  system, dependency versions, exact options, color space, alpha mode, mip
  policy, and determinism status;
- encoded bytes, compression ratio, encode time, CPU decode time, GPU upload
  time, time to first usable frame, peak host memory, estimated texture
  memory, and steady-state sampling behavior;
- cold and warm observations, warm-up and repetition counts, median, and tail
  measurements rather than one favorable timing;
- pixel and structural error against the lossless reference, transparency,
  edge behavior, title/legend legibility, thin-line retention, field-opacity
  preservation, and pass-specific moiré observations;
- Float64-to-Float32 projected and screen-pixel displacement, reverse
  residual, status, and candidate-set agreement at cuts and overlaps; and
- unsupported operations, failures, warnings, and human visual disposition
  without silently dropping a product or machine.

PNG remains the visual control, not necessarily the preferred transfer
format. Flat water, translucent fields, fine networks, text, boundaries, and
high-frequency curve work must be reviewed separately; one aggregate score
cannot hide pass-specific damage.

## Reassessment

Cartofreako is already useful to an agent as an atlas **selection** system and
as a bounded, one-off **experiment** system. It is not yet a general agentic
research-iteration system.

The current evidence is substantial:

- a checked catalog exposes 211 standard products across 32 pass IDs;
- deterministic requests and decision receipts select existing artifacts;
- all six projection families have candidate-aware forward and reverse APIs;
- 1080p derivatives support preview-first inspection without opening very
  large SVGs, while the full PNG, layered SVG, PDF, 44-inch-leading-edge, and
  A0 products remain authoritative;
- the Marshall Islands v01 workflow turns five projection hypotheses into
  reproducible PNG comparison plates;
- the Majuro evidence pass exercises six carrier projections without claiming
  that a planetary carrier is the local analytical grid; and
- the PurpleAir-interface and water-debris experiments demonstrate explicit
  `exploration-only` products, missing-evidence labels, source periods, and
  non-promotion boundaries.

The gap is orchestration. The Marshall Islands renderer hard-codes one useful
sequence; the artifact selector chooses only already-known products; and each
new research family defines its own manifest, generator, checks, contact
sheet, and review language. An agent can assist the present workflow, but
cannot yet express and run the whole sequence through a stable,
implementation-neutral contract.

## Proposed operating model

Use two connected loops rather than one unconstrained “make a map” command.

### Loop A — select and compare existing atlas products

1. Normalize the research question into a checked request: geography,
   interval, evidence themes, audience, allowed lifecycle classes, output
   budget, and publication boundary.
2. Query the artifact catalog and projection-capability registry.
3. Return one-to-five projection, slice, and plate candidates with explicit
   reasons, exclusions, source dates, cuts, inverse behavior, and evidence
   limitations.
4. Render only previews or 1080p comparison sheets first.
5. Record a human review disposition before producing full PNG, SVG, PDF, or
   print derivatives.

### Loop B — develop a bounded new analysis

1. Convert an evidence gap from Loop A into an experiment specification.
2. Inventory candidate sources by evidence role: observation, scenario,
   interpretation, context, provenance, or `UNAVAILABLE`.
3. Require an explicit acquisition decision for credentials, terms, large
   downloads, personal data, or non-redistributable sources. Generation never
   implies that authorization.
4. Build a small deterministic canary and a contact sheet, then run numeric,
   source, lifecycle, and visual checks.
5. Let a reviewer accept, reject, or request one controlled revision. Change
   one named variable per iteration where practical.
6. Preserve an iteration lineage and result receipt. Promotion to optional or
   standard is a later, separate decision; GitHub release and UCB AAO/S3
   deposit remain human-invoked operations.

This division lets an agent recommend and iterate without allowing a fluent
answer to become source evidence, community authorization, or release
authority.

## First experimental-analysis queue

The agent should propose small, evidence-bounded increments in this order:

1. **Marshall Islands evidence comparison.** Join the current Majuro
   topobathymetry and inundation panels to selected reef, temperature,
   particulate, and source-timed atmosphere context without merging their
   dates or evidence roles.
2. **Anthropocene water debris v02.** Keep the five real 2018 depth stations
   as observations, then test one newly reviewed debris source at a time.
   Never infer a garbage-patch boundary or relabel the 2025/2026 atlas edition
   as the observation year.
3. **Atmosphere sequence.** Compare the optional JAXA snapshot, standard
   particulate and temperature editions, and the synthetic PurpleAir
   interface as separate panels. A real PurpleAir adapter remains blocked on
   source-use review and observed-data provenance.
4. **Worldwide reef sequence.** Combine the released 2011 reef-threat
   geometry with current ocean-heat or bleaching evidence only after the new
   source's resolution, interval, uncertainty, redistribution, and missing
   semantics are recorded.
5. **Local atoll evidence tier.** Add shoreline, freshwater, infrastructure,
   current reef, and ocean-heat sources independently. Community or regional
   review is a promotion gate, not a task the agent can satisfy.

Each experiment should start with PNG-only previews or a contact sheet. Large
SVG/PDF/print products are generated only after the visual direction and
evidence boundary are reviewed.

## Contracts required

Four new checked contracts would generalize the existing canaries.

### `atlas-research-request-v1`

Minimum fields:

- research question, geographic focus and bounding geometry;
- time interval and whether comparison across unlike periods is permitted;
- evidence themes and allowed lifecycle classes;
- intended audience and purpose (`planetary-context`, `regional-comparison`,
  `local-analysis`, `exhibition`, or `runtime`);
- candidate limit from one to five, output budget, and preview-only default;
- allowed local/public inputs and explicit network/acquisition authorization;
- community, privacy, license, publication, and release boundaries; and
- catalog, runtime, source-commit, and house-style identities.

### `atlas-recommendation-receipt-v1`

For every candidate, record its projection, slice, plate IDs, score components,
distortion/topology rationale, reverse behavior, source periods, lifecycle,
limitations, and rejection reasons. The receipt must distinguish observed
metadata from inferred suitability and retain any human override.

### `atlas-experiment-v1`

Record the hypothesis, parent request/receipt hashes, source inventory,
evidence roles, transformations, projection/slice matrix, layer defaults,
opacity/title rules, render recipes, deterministic seeds, output formats,
resource limits, QA fixtures, and an unconditional initial
`exploration-only` lifecycle.

### `atlas-experiment-result-v1`

Record resolved input and toolchain hashes, outputs, warnings, forward/reverse
residuals, missing evidence, visual-review status, reproduction command,
parent/child iteration IDs, and promotion disposition. A separate human
review receipt should name the reviewer action without implying community
consent, scientific validation, endorsement, or permission to publish.

## Proposed command surface

Keep pure JSON commands beneath thin Make wrappers:

```sh
make propose-atlas-research \
  RESEARCH_REQUEST=work/marshall-islands-request.json

make render-atlas-experiment \
  EXPERIMENT_SPEC=work/marshall-islands-v02.json

make check-atlas-experiment \
  EXPERIMENT_RESULT=work/marshall-islands-v02-result.json

make package-atlas-review \
  EXPERIMENT_RESULT=work/marshall-islands-v02-result.json
```

The proposal target is read-only and may not fetch. The render target may use
only inputs authorized by its checked specification. The check target must be
offline. The review package contains previews, hashes, source/evidence tables,
limitations, and a blank review receipt; it does not publish.

## Recommendation logic

The selector needs a checked projection-suitability registry rather than an
opaque model ranking. Its features should include:

- geographic containment, relevant seam crossings, visible area, and label
  budget;
- full carrier versus native-cell or geographic preclip;
- purpose-specific continuity, distance/area limitations, aspect ratio, and
  portrait/landscape fit;
- forward/reverse availability, ambiguity, component/face qualification, and
  residual evidence;
- compatible existing passes and source periods;
- evidence lifecycle, license, uncertainty, missing-data semantics, and
  community-review state; and
- output cost from thumbnail through authoritative print.

The deterministic score should produce an explanation. An agent may summarize
or challenge it, but cannot silently replace it with an unrecorded preference.

## Safety and authority boundaries

- No target reads secrets from a prompt, writes credentials into a fixture,
  accepts provider terms, or authorizes an external source.
- No experiment enters `make all`, a release, or AAO merely because it renders
  successfully or appears in `all-experiments`.
- No synthetic PurpleAir marker, inferred debris geometry, interpolated
  shoreline, false zero, or absent observation may be described as measured.
- No agent claims community consent, local interpretation, scientific
  validation, endorsement, patentability, or publication authority.
- Current public S3 v13 assets and current local post-v13 products must remain
  distinguishable; a local result cannot invent a public URL.
- Every authoritative print product retains its projection-specific ratio,
  44-inch leading edge, and A0 path. Preview and 1080p products remain
  derivatives.
