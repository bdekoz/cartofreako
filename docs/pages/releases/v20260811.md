## Stage 15 result

- The standard Anthropocene family now contains explicit complete-2025 and
  partial-2026 particulate editions plus the two accepted CPC temperature
  editions. The unqualified observation artifact is removed; compatibility
  targets build the year-qualified pair.
- Pass-status and standard-artifact manifests distinguish the four standard,
  default-generated, accepted-experimental Anthropocene pass IDs from
  exploration-only research products.
- The frozen Stage 14 catalog remains historical evidence while the live
  standard graph grows from 205/31 to 211/32. Lossless 2048 × 1024 landscape
  and 1024 × 2048 portrait controls retain their frozen parent identities.
- A checked local CDN/WASM candidate demonstrates versioned indexes, runtime
  media/cache policy, and release separation without publishing a completion
  marker.
- The visually approved Majuro canary is extended into six projection
  compositions and 24 local SVG/PDF/full-PNG/thumbnail artifacts. Checked
  source contracts keep the planetary carrier, USGS observation-derived
  topobathymetry, and inundation scenario separate.
- Twelve PurpleAir-interface SVGs use default-visible synthetic QA anchors at
  60% opacity and contain no sensor or measurement claim.
- Twelve water-debris SVGs render only five reviewed 2018 depth stations;
  other source families remain context-only or unavailable. No garbage-patch
  polygon or invented depth field is introduced.
- `make all-experiments` registers and builds seven non-release families:
  lossless GPU controls, local consumer layout, Majuro canary, five Marshall
  Islands speculation PNGs, the full Majuro pass, PurpleAir interface, and
  water-debris experiment. It cannot fetch, authorize, promote, release, or
  upload.

The included `output/` contact sheets and speculative PNGs are review evidence,
not released atlas masters. The ignored `assets.generated/` tree remains a
local render workspace and is not attached to this source release.

## Stage 16 transition

The [Stage 16 ledger](../../development/stage-16.md) receives every explicitly
deferred Stage 15 track: compressed GPU formats, hardware timing, picking
masks, Float32 audits, additional consumers, GPU promotion decisions, and
remaining atoll evidence tiers. It also records the approved two-loop agentic
atlas program.

The first agentic canary will compare source-timed atmosphere products; a
water-debris v02 iteration follows. The initial Marshall Islands
recommendation fixture ranks Myriahedral Pacific, Dymaxion, Cahill–Keyes,
local azimuthal equidistant, and Star-X by research purpose rather than naming
one universally best projection. Approval records future scope; no generic
orchestrator or Stage 16 product is claimed in this release.

## Publication boundary

GitHub source publication and UCB AAO preservation remain separate concepts
and separate Make targets. This release uses only `release-github`.
`release-ucb-aao-s3` remains top-level, interactive-only, and delegated to
`alpha60-clusterops/bin/load-s3-aao`. It is not a dependency of generation,
experiments, checks, source release, or any unattended agent workflow.
