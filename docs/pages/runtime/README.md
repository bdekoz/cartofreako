The authoritative art and archive products remain the projection-ratio,
44-inch-leading-edge and A0 print families. Runtime and 1080p products are
delivery derivatives, not replacements for those masters.

The Stage 14 v1 consumer canary now supplies 24 exact 1920 × 1080
PNG/lossless-WebP pairs, a checked artifact catalog and schema, all-family
affine screen picking, a headless Canvas example, and a flat WebGL/Three.js
plane record. Full-matrix cataloging and GPU-specific products remain future
work.
