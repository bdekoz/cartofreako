This source-only GitHub release completes Stage 14 reverse-projection support
across Cartofreako's six registered projection families. It does not publish a
generated-assets v14 archive and does not authorize or perform a UCB Active
Archive Object Storage deposit over S3.

## Stage 14 result

- Cahill–Keyes, AuthaGraph, Dymaxion, Myriahedral, Star-X, and Voronoi expose
  forward and reverse projection through the common native and WebAssembly
  runtime.
- AuthaGraph and Dymaxion accept face-qualified reverse requests where their
  unfolded layouts overlap. Myriahedral and Voronoi preserve explicit facet
  identity. Candidate enumeration avoids silently choosing among valid
  inverse branches.
- Star-X models the main four-quadrant carrier and the reconstructed Antarctic
  cap as two components. Its cap is projection-native, begins at the fixed
  `60°S` cutoff, stays above the other quadrants in paint order, and no longer
  depends on Natural Earth geometry. The reconstructed boundary has sufficient
  lower-page clearance.
- Browser consumers receive immutable component metadata, single and batch
  reverse methods, typed-array paths, worker support, and D3 candidate access.
- Two stale pre-Izzi bathymetry curve includes found by the full release gate
  now use their canonical Izzi headers.

The [forward/reverse API](../forward-reverse-projection-api.md), individual
projection implementation notes, and the
[Stage 14 convergence ledger](../converge-generation-14.md) record the
mathematics, ambiguity contracts, tolerances, and implementation decisions.

## Publication boundary

GitHub publication and UCB AAO preservation are separate concepts and separate
Make targets. This release uses only `release-github`. The
`release-ucb-aao-s3` target is top-level and interactive-only, requires an
explicit reviewed profile and staged tree, and is not a dependency of any
GitHub, build, check, or generation target. It is a thin wrapper over
`alpha60-clusterops/bin/load-s3-aao`; Cartofreako does not reimplement shared
S3 transport. See the [release runbook](README.md).
