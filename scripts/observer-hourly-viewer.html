“Above the horizon” means elevation ≥ 0° from San Francisco (37.7749° N, 122.4194° W; 16 m). It is not a higher position on this unfolded sky map or a guarantee of naked-eye visibility. The dashed previous horizon is a comparison boundary, not a satellite trajectory.

Each hour is calculated independently with SGP4. The same catalog bytes are retained throughout; seven-day element-age and six-hour future-epoch limits remain active, so the eligible population can change. UTC-based model sidereal orientation follows the renderer’s existing GMST convention.

Playback holds each hourly state for two seconds and stops at the end. The 24 sample times span 23 elapsed hours. The +24-hour proof retains the full-day endpoint; the last frame does not physically loop back to the first. Fine names are available in the vector plate and at viewer zoom; the video overview hides them.
