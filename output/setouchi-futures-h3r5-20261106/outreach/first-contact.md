# Unsent introduction to Hajime Narukawa

Suggested route: https://narukawa-lab.jp/contact/

**Subject:** Cartofreako × Setouchi — AuthaGraph, print, and a public atlas studio

Dear Professor Narukawa,

I am Benjamin De Kosnik, an artist and engineer developing Cartofreako, an atlas project connecting maps, networks, and printmaking. For a proposed public atlas studio at Setouchi 2028, I am pairing World and Japan views of water, atmosphere, temperature records, fiber networks, and satellites with activities in drawing, folding, and printing.

Your work with AuthaGraph, especially the connection between geometry and making in the AuthaGraph Stamp project, is closely related to the questions I hope visitors will explore. I would value your perspective on using map projection as a tool for a public workshop.

Would you be open to a brief conversation or a short exchange by email? The project overview is here:
https://benjamin.dekosnik.com/futures/setouchi-2028/

Best wishes,
Benjamin De Kosnik
