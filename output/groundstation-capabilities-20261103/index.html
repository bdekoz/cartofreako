Groundstation capability studies

Three radial fills on the same Setouchi AuthaGraph map, each with a different Randoma11y pair. Triangles sit at the centers of hexagonal cells; their areas scale with the largest complete listed antenna bank’s aperture. Records, sizes and placement match across all three. Colors express design intent; the ink shortage and metallic paper make printed hues uncertain.

538 antenna records can be sized; 52 have unknown sizes. The 590 antenna records include planned and retired installations. The 66 PoPs and 124 supplied lines remain separate. This is a hardware proxy, not measured throughput or a verified census of operating sites.

Solid, dashed and dotted triangle outlines distinguish source-reported operation, unconfirmed/planned status and retirement. A hollow central square marks unknown size. The categorical patterns are not antenna counts or radiation diagrams. Open the vector PDFs to inspect fine patterns at print size.
