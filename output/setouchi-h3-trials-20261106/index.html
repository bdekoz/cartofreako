Atmosphere and 2026 temperature records, World and Japan. These trials keep the original dates, sampled source centers, colors, and line weights. H3r3 is the saved website baseline. H3r5 and H3r8 show actual finer cell geometry.

Finer cells do not add observations. Gaps can mean there was no sampled center in that cell; source-pixel footprints are not filled. At r8, source spacing and fixed stroke widths can dominate the appearance. The atmosphere sampling cap remains 720 per raster axis; temperature uses the original 0.5° grid.

Maps and source legends are separate 44-inch sheets. Dense data layers render at 300 dpi; coastlines, solar contours, and text remain vectors. PDFs are self-contained; keep the adjacent layers folder with each SVG. Click a preview for the full-resolution PNG, or download its PDF/SVG.
