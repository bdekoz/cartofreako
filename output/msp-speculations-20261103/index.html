MSP print speculations

Three studies for the Epson Stylus Pro 9000 on metallic glossy paper, responding to reported low cyan/light magenta, a violet cast in grays, and stronger results with dark backgrounds. These are unmeasured color hypotheses.

Setouchi AuthaGraph framing: 43.5-inch image on a 44-inch sheet, +4.35-inch printed horizontal move and lower-left Antarctic land inset. Each map has a separate legend with attribution. Global Starlink NORAD IDs print at 9 pt.
