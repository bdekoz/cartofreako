MSP print speculations

MSP speculation 4 is available in all four Randoma11y pairs, each with groundstation, orbital global and orbital observer map/legend companions. Studies 1–3 remain available for comparison. Orbital vendors retain their distinct source colors; the pairs control backgrounds, type and bright groundstation fiber lines.

Epson Stylus Pro 9000 on metallic glossy paper: cyan and light magenta are reported low, grays print violet, and dark backgrounds work better. These are intended RGB design choices, without a measured prediction of the printed hue.

Setouchi AuthaGraph framing: 43.5-inch image on a 44-inch sheet, +4.35-inch printed horizontal move and lower-left Antarctic land inset beneath the data. Each map has a separate legend with attribution. Global Starlink NORAD IDs print at 9 pt in studies 1–3 and 6 pt in every MSP 4 palette.
