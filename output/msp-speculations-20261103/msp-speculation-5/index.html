MSP speculation 5

MSP speculation 5 is available in all four Randoma11y pairs, each with groundstation, orbital global, orbital observer and astronomy all-sky map/legend companions. Orbital vendors and astronomy symbols retain their distinct source colors; the pairs control backgrounds, type and bright groundstation fiber lines.

All MSP studies, including the previous revisions.

Epson Stylus Pro 9000 on metallic glossy paper: cyan and light magenta are reported low, grays print violet, and dark backgrounds work better. These are intended RGB design choices, without a measured prediction of the printed hue.

Setouchi AuthaGraph framing: 43.5-inch image on a 44-inch sheet, +4.35-inch printed horizontal move and lower-left Antarctic land inset beneath terrestrial data. Each map has a separate legend with attribution. Global Starlink NORAD IDs print at 9 pt in studies 1–3 and 6 pt in every MSP 4 and MSP 5 palette.

MSP speculation 5 / palette 1 · Wine / amber

Randoma11y pair: ground #240413, type and groundstation fiber lines #fcc553. Orbital markers retain their vendor colors. Source sRGB contrast 12.03:1; printed contrast and hue remain unmeasured.

Petal capability triangles on the groundstation map. Both orbital maps use 1.5× Starlink sunbursts; global Starlink IDs use 6 pt type beneath all satellite markers. Antarctic land paints below labels and marks.

Observer Starlink names use 6 pt type; other satellite names stay hidden. Global ground tracks use 2× their original line width.

Astronomy all sky retains its 29 October 2026, 20:00 UTC source positions, symbol colors and 35 labels. Sky labels use 12 pt type on the full-size sheet. Its background follows this color set, with light typography and a separate source legend.
